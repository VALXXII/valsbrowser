## What appears here, and why

Third-party names, logos and screenshots are used only as examples, to identify the service a
feature talks to or to show how a page looks in the browser:

- **Search engines** (`assets/engines`): Google, Bing, Brave, DuckDuckGo, Ecosia, Qwant. Shown
  next to the search engine the user picks.
- **Website icons** (`assets/sites`, `assets/popular-sites.txt`): the favicons of well-known
  websites, shown in address bar suggestions.
- **Assistant logos** (`assets/chat-panel/icons.js`): Claude (Anthropic) and Codex (OpenAI).
  Shown to identify the assistant the user connects with their own account.
- **Other browsers** (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and others): named, and sometimes shown by
  their icon, in the import and onboarding screens and in their design mock-ups.
- **Screenshots and mock-ups** (`design/`, `tests/`): some pictures show third-party websites or
  interfaces as sample content.
